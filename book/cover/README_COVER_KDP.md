## 2. Author Biography (About the Author)

**Thomas Riebl** is a thinker and theoretical researcher based in Luxembourg. He is the originator of *The Conative-Integrative Framework (CIF)*, the formulation of the *6th Axiom of Consciousness*, and the mathematical formalization of *Temporal Depth Agency* bridging Active Inference with Integrated Information Theory. His research explores the intersection of computational neuroscience, non-dual metaphysics, behavioral genetics, and synthetic cognition.
